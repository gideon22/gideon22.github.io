Privacy policy

home-assistant is a private assistant that runs on a computer in the owner's home. It is used only by the members of that household, who authorize it with their own Google accounts.

What it accesses

  • Gmail and Google Calendar of the household members who sign in, with read-only permissions.
  • It uses this data only to give those same people reminders and summaries.

What it does not do

  • It does not send, delete or change email or calendar data.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone.
  • It does not use Google user data for advertising or to train general-purpose AI models.

Storage and removal

Authorization tokens and data stay on the owner's home computer. Any user can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
